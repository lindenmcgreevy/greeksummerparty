Travel

To reach Paros, you can either take a quick domestic flight from Athens or enjoy the scenic ferry ride. Both are great options, but we recommend booking in advance as they tend to fill up quickly during the summer months.

Fly ATH – PAS

For the fastest trip, you can fly into Athens International Airport "Eleftherios Venizelos" (ATH) and take a quick 40-minute connecting flight to Paros Airport (PAS). Once you land, it's a 15-minute drive north to the town of Parikia or 30 minutes to Naoussa. Taxis are readily available at the airport entrance, or you can pick up a rental car if you're planning to explore the island on your own.

Ferry from Piraeus to Paros

To catch the ferry, you'll head to Piraeus Harbor (Gate E9). We recommend using Ferryscanner to book your tickets online in advance. The high-speed ferry takes about 2h 45m, and will drop you off directly in the heart of Parikia. Make sure to arrive at the port 30–45 mins before your departure time to account for traffic and boarding.

Accommodation

We recommend staying on the west side of the island, between Pounta and Pasaporos.
